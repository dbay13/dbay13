SCRIPTORIUM

A clickable prototype of the iPhone app: a quiet notebook dressed as a medieval manuscript.

  • Press the red wax seal to write a new scroll. The first line is its title.
  • Tap a checkbox to tick it off. Return adds the next item.
  • Swipe a scroll left to burn it, right to seal it.
  • Open Letters Unsent and break its seal.
  • In a scroll, tap the flame to begin a Vigil.
  • Open the Chamber (top right) to change parchment and ink.

Face ID is simulated. Scrolls you write are kept in this browser only.
