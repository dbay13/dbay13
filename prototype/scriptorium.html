SCRIPTORIUM

A private diary, sealed in wax. Everything on the right works.

  • Begin today's page from the card at the top. A question waits when the page is blank.
  • Tap a gilt day in the calendar to open it; tap an empty day to write for it.
  • Mark the day's humour with the quartered circle in the toolbar.
  • Open Letters Unsent and break its seal.
  • In the Chamber, become a Patron: type thine initial and watch thy seal being made.
  • Turn on "Seal the diary on opening", then switch tabs and come back.

Face ID and purchases are simulated; no money is taken. Pages are kept in this browser only.
