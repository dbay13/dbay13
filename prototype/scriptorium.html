SCRIPTORIUM

A quiet notebook dressed as a medieval manuscript. Everything on the right works.

  • Press the wax seal to begin a new scroll. Its first line is the title.
  • Tap a box to tick it off. Return adds the next item.
  • Swipe a scroll left to burn it, right to seal it.
  • Open Letters Unsent and break its seal.
  • Inside a scroll, light the candle to begin a vigil.
  • Open the Chamber to change parchment and wax.

Face ID is simulated. Scrolls you write are kept in this browser only.
